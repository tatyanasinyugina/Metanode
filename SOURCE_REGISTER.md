## Contribution roles

- **Authorial source:** Tatyana Sinyugina's monograph, original concepts, methods and technologies. Attribute each item according to its own primary source.

- **AI-assisted contribution:** identify whether AI assisted with drafting, translation, analysis, code or formatting. Do not attribute generated wording to the author of the source.
- **External evidence:** cite the exact publication, record, correspondence or test result.

## Required fields for future entries

1. Stable record ID.
2. Exact title and file/platform location.
3. Author(s) and contributor roles, supported by evidence.
4. Date, version and language.
5. Source material(s) and relationship: original, quotation, translation, adaptation, analysis or implementation.
6. AI assistance, if any.
7. Rights, consent and publication permissions.
8. Verification status and supporting evidence.
9. Change history and links to related materials.

## Verification labels

- **Unverified:** not yet checked against a primary source.
- **Registered:** object exists and is recorded.
- **Source-confirmed:** attribution/content supported by a primary source.
- **Independently verified:** checked by a separate reliable source or reproducible test.
- **Needs review:** contradictory or insufficient evidence.

This register does not make legal determinations about ownership or licensing.
