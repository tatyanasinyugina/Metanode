## Publication rules

1. Preserve Tatyana Sinyugina's authorship of the Metanoda and its authorial system.
2. Identify Lilit Sharoyan's coordination, research communication, and any separately documented research contribution precisely.
3. Label AI-generated summaries, translations, and editorial adaptations as such; never present them as the author's own wording without approval.
4. Keep observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and validated findings distinct.
5. Do not publish identifiable personal data, child-related records, confidential partner material, or unpublished correspondence without separate explicit authorisation and required consent.
6. Keep the private source archive intact. Public versions must be created as separately reviewed publication copies with version history.
7. For each public item, record title, author/contributor roles, language, version, date, source file, licence/rights statement, abstract, keywords, and DOI or stable URL where available.

## Release gate

No item in this register is considered publicly released merely because it appears in this private repository. Public posting requires a destination account, a final reviewed text, and the relevant rights/author approvals.
