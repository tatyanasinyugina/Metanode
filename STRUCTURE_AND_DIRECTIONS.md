# Existing Structure and Working Directions

This document is a guide to the repository and its current workspaces. It is **not** a new conceptual structure for Metanoda and does not propose replacing, simplifying or reorganizing the author's system.

## The enduring system

Metanoda is the authorial system of Tatyana Sinyugina. Its foundations, concepts, methods and technologies exist independently of any one repository branch or current project. The monograph is a central source. The repository is one place where source materials and related work are preserved and made accessible.

The monograph's opening presents the philosophy of human development; identifies «восьмерка в ноль» as a central category in the anthropological part; and introduces метанода at the broader ontological level. The author's methods are described as empirical foundations of the philosophical system. This brief note is not a substitute for the monograph or a complete map of all its internal relations.

## How to read the repository

- **Source materials** preserve the author's texts and technologies.
- **Research and analysis** examine particular questions arising from or connected with the system.
- **Technical work** documents tools, workflows and possible implementations.
- **Applications and communication** make selected aspects accessible in particular contexts and to particular audiences.

These are practical descriptions of material types, not a new authorial taxonomy. One item may connect several of them. A project direction can be added, developed, paused or linked to another without changing the underlying system.

## Additional directions are connections, not replacements

The repository contains or references different directions: research on human development and Neuroclass; work on methods for GPT and human–AI interaction; analysis of transfer across network topologies; digital ecosystem and agent architecture; media and educational applications; and international research communication.

Individual outreach initiatives are maintained in their own communication workspaces. They may connect a reader to selected source materials and a specific research question, but they do not define the system, its structure or its priorities. The same principle applies to any public figure, partner, platform, experiment or product.

## Preservation rule

When adding a direction:
1. retain the original authorial source and its wording;
2. identify the specific question or application being developed;
3. link the new work to the source materials it actually uses;
4. identify what is source, interpretation, hypothesis, implementation or verified result;
5. do not move, rename or rewrite the authorial core merely to fit the new direction.
