### Third: follow one relevant technical question

6. **Digital Twin Transfer Analysis.**  
   [Open the analysis](https://github.com/tatyanasinyugina/Metanode/blob/main/Metanoda_Digital_Twin_Transfer_Analysis_EN%202.md)

   A possible point of connection to current AI and compute discussions is the question of what remains stable, what changes, and what new capabilities appear when a system is transferred between different network topologies.

   The relevant research questions include:
   - Which properties persist across topological changes?
   - How can topology effects be separated from weights, memory/state, interface, prompts, and compute?
   - What would count as a genuinely new, measurable capability rather than a prompt or resource effect?
   - What controls, baselines, and replications are needed?

   These are research questions and hypotheses, not established results. The analysis should be evaluated on its methods and evidence.

## 4. The shortest honest introduction

METANODA is Tatyana Sinyugina's authorial system. The monograph and original methods are the primary sources. The project includes several related research and technical directions; one current question concerns what is preserved or newly possible when systems move between network topologies. The question can be made testable by separating topology from model weights, state, interface, prompts, and compute, then comparing controlled outcomes.
