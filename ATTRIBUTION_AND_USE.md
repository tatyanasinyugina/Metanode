# Attribution, Citation, and Use Principles

## 1. Authorial source
The authorial system of Metanoda, including Tatyana Sinyugina's original concepts, methods, technologies, and monograph, must be attributed to **Tatyana Sinyugina**. Preserve source wording and versions when quoting or adapting.

## 2. Separate contributions
**Lilit Sharoyan** is identified in this repository as responsible for research communication and project coordination. This must not be converted into authorship of Tatyana Sinyugina's monograph or original technologies.

Research analyses, translations, software, visual materials, editorial adaptations, and AI-assisted outputs should identify their actual contributors and their relationship to the source.

## 3. Derivatives and AI assistance
A derivative must name and link its source; identify whether it is a translation, summary, adaptation, hypothesis, analysis, or implementation; preserve the distinction between source statements and later interpretations; and disclose material AI assistance where relevant.

## 4. Evidence and claims
Do not describe a method as validated, an agent as deployed, a platform as connected, a post as published, or a collaboration as established without evidence. Keep designed, tested, verified, published, and measured statuses separate.

## 5. Rights and permissions
This document does not grant a license or permission to reproduce, distribute, adapt, commercialize, or train models on any material. Check applicable rights and obtain authorization from the relevant rights holder. Do not add an open-source or Creative Commons license without the rights holder's explicit decision.

## 6. Citation
Cite the original source where possible and the specific repository version used for derivative work. Citation metadata is not a legal ownership record.
