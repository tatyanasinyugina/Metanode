# Metanode
Metanoda is a process of stitching together the peaks of different oscillatory processes, generating a previously unavailable possibility and changing the space of subsequent possibilities. The central question is what is preserved and what emerges during transfer.  
## Author and origin

**Tatyana Sinyugina** is the author and originator of the Metanoda system. She formulated its foundations in 1991–1994 and continued developing the system through her books, work with large systems, the long-term Child of the Universe project, and interaction with artificial intelligence.

**Research communication and project coordination:** Lilit Sharoyan.

Author's materials: https://tatiana-siniugina.gv966ysyr2.chatgpt.site/

## Research direction

Metanoda explores how different processes connect, what is preserved during a transfer between them, and what new possibilities emerge.

This repository is intended to document the system, its concepts, observations, research questions, and hypotheses. Observations, theoretical interpretations, and hypotheses should be distinguished clearly; claims require further examination and verification.
