# Metanoda — Tatyana Sinyugina's authorial system

**Metanoda** is the authorial philosophical and research system developed by **Tatyana Sinyugina**. Its foundations are not defined by any one experiment, AI model, product, communication campaign, or repository branch. This repository preserves the monograph and related methodological, analytical, and technical materials.

> “Metanoda is a process of stitching together the peaks of different oscillatory processes, generating a previously unavailable possibility and changing the space of subsequent possibilities.”
>
> — Tatyana Sinyugina, quoted in the [Digital Twin Transfer Analysis](./Metanoda_Digital_Twin_Transfer_Analysis_EN%202.md).

The central task is to understand what is preserved, transformed, and newly produced when processes interact — while distinguishing the author's philosophical propositions from observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and independently tested results.

## 3. Comparative and research analyses

These documents develop particular questions connected with the system. They are analytical work, not replacements for the monograph.

The relationship between Metanoda, artificial intelligence, and consciousness is an empirical research question. The repository does not present the framework as a validated consciousness detector or as proof that current AI systems are conscious. Claims about machine experience or persistent capabilities require operational definitions, controlled comparisons, and independent tests.

- **[Six Stages: Student, GPT, and Autistic Learner](./Six_Stages_Student_GPT_Autistic_Learner_EN.md)** — a comparative analysis of proposed correspondences between the author's sequence and three forms of activity. It explicitly distinguishes observable actions and outputs from claims about inner experience or identical mechanisms. Session observations, interpretations, and hypotheses must remain separate.
- **[Transferring a Digital Twin Across Different Network Topologies](./Metanoda_Digital_Twin_Transfer_Analysis_EN%202.md)** — a research analysis connecting network-topology transfer with questions raised by Metanoda. It distinguishes the cited preprint, the proposed formalization, experimental suggestions, and findings. The proposed criteria and hypotheses are not presented as proof that Metanoda has been demonstrated.

## 6. Evidence and preservation principles

When reading or extending this repository, keep the following distinctions explicit:

1. **Authorial source** — the monograph and original concepts, methods, and technologies, attributed to Tatyana Sinyugina according to their source records.
2. **Translation or format copy** — another language or file format of an existing source; not a new method.
3. **Adaptation** — a source method operationalized for a particular model, task, or audience.
4. **Analysis** — a reasoned examination of a particular question, with its sources and limits identified.
5. **Observation** — a recorded event or output, kept separate from its interpretation.
6. **Hypothesis** — a proposition requiring a test.
7. **Verified result** — a result supported by a documented and reproducible procedure.
8. **Technical design or implementation** — a proposed or built tool; deployment must be evidenced separately.

Original texts, definitions, methods, and technologies must not be rewritten to fit a temporary project or audience. Derivative work belongs in clearly identified files and working branches, with links to the exact source versions. Corrections to an original source require a new proposed version and the author's explicit approval. A Git commit or merge is a technical event, not automatic authorial acceptance.
